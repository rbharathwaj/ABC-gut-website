05 · Action Plan
Personalized Recommendations
1
Increase Butyrate Production — Critical Priority
Goal: Raise butyrate from 1.1 to 5.1+ μmol/g within 8–12 weeks. Prioritize resistant starch as the primary dietary lever. Add tributyrin supplement and increase intact whole grains and cruciferous vegetables daily. Inulin-rich foods (chicory root, Jerusalem artichoke, garlic, asparagus) support Faecalibacterium, your key butyrate producer.
Resistant starch 15–30g/day Tributyrin 300mg/day Farro · barley · quinoa Broccoli · Brussels sprouts · cabbage Chicory root · garlic · asparagus
2
Restore Bifidobacterium Levels
Goal: Increase from 6.1% to >10% for optimal immune function. A multi-strain Bifidobacterium probiotic combined with GOS prebiotic provides targeted support. Fermented dairy (if tolerated) introduces additional beneficial strains. Eliminate artificial sweeteners — evidence shows saccharin, aspartame, and sucralose directly reduce Bifidobacterium abundance.
B. longum · B. breve · B. infantis (10B+ CFU) GOS prebiotic 5g/day Kefir · yogurt daily Eliminate artificial sweeteners
3
Reduce Proteobacteria & Inflammation
Goal: Lower Proteobacteria from 6.1% to <4% to reduce inflammatory burden. Omega-3 fatty acids and daily polyphenols are the frontline approach. Temporarily reduce animal fats (promotes sulfate-reducing bacteria). Consider berberine (900mg/day) or oregano oil under practitioner guidance for antimicrobial botanical support.
Omega-3 2–3g EPA/DHA/day Green tea · berries · pomegranate Dark chocolate (polyphenols) Berberine 900mg/day (practitioner) Reduce animal fat & red meat
Lifestyle Foundations
Exercise
≥150 min/week of moderate movement increases microbiome diversity by 20%+ and directly enhances SCFA production.
Sleep
7–9 hours with consistent timing supports circadian–microbiome alignment. Disrupted sleep alters microbial composition.
Stress Management
Chronic stress directly reduces Bifidobacterium and increases intestinal permeability. Meditation, yoga, or breathing exercises stabilize the gut–brain axis.
